ytpipe

A personal tool for planning, reviewing and publishing videos to YouTube channels owned by its operator.

What it is

ytpipe is a single-operator command-line tool. It helps one person research topics, draft and review scripts, assemble videos and publish them to their own YouTube channels on a fixed schedule. It is not a hosted service, it has no user accounts, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads the operator's own channel details to confirm which channel it is acting for.
  • Reads public search and video statistics to inform topic research.
  • Uploads videos to, and manages metadata on, channels the operator owns.
  • Reads the operator's own YouTube Analytics.

Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published. Videos that contain synthetic or AI-assisted media are disclosed as such to YouTube.
