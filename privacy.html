Privacy policy

ytpipe. Last updated 9 September 2026.

Who this covers

ytpipe is a personal command-line tool operated by one individual for their own YouTube channels. It has no other users. The only Google Account it accesses is the operator's own, authorised by that person through Google's standard OAuth consent flow.

What is accessed

  • Channel identity and settings for channels the operator owns.
  • Publicly available YouTube search results and video statistics.
  • Video upload and metadata management on the operator's own channels.
  • YouTube Analytics for the operator's own channels.

Where it is stored

All data stays on the operator's own computer. OAuth tokens are written to a permission-restricted local directory. Job records, drafts and generated files are held in a local database and local folders. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by ytpipe, because no such server exists.

What is shared

Nothing is sold, rented or shared. Data is transmitted only to the services the tool is configured to call in order to do its job, namely Google's YouTube APIs and the AI model providers used to draft text and generate media. Their handling of that data is governed by their own terms. Google user data is never used to train any model, and is never passed to a model provider.

Retention and deletion

Local data persists until the operator deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token. Deleting the local secrets directory removes every stored credential.

Limited use

ytpipe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
